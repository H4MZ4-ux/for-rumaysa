My Dearest Rumaysa

A letter written just for you

Rumaysa,

I have sat down to write this more times than I can count. Each time the words felt too small for everything you mean to me. So tonight I am writing it all — the long version, the true version, the one that has been living quietly in my chest every single day.

I love you. Not in the casual way people say it when they are happy or when the moment feels right. I love you in the deep, steady way that settles into the bones. I love the sound of your name. I love the way the world softens when you are near. I love the quiet between us as much as the laughter. I love the way you see things, the way you care, the way you simply exist and somehow make ordinary hours feel like something worth remembering.

There are days when the thought of you is the first thing that greets me and the last thing that stays with me. On those days I catch myself smiling at nothing, only to realize it is because something small reminded me of you — a song, a color, a sentence I wish I could say out loud to you right then. You have become the place my mind returns to when it wants to rest.

I love how you make me want to be better without ever demanding it. I love the safety I feel when I am honest with you. I love that I can be quiet with you and it never feels empty. I love the future that starts forming in my head the moment I think of your face — not a grand, impossible future, just a gentle one filled with ordinary mornings, shared meals, long conversations, and the kind of peace that only comes from knowing someone truly sees you.

If I could take every soft feeling I have ever had and gather them into one place, they would still fall short of what you are to me. You are the reason some days feel lighter. You are the reason I believe in slow, careful love. You are the person I want to keep choosing, again and again, for as many years as we are given.

I know life is not always gentle. There will be hard days and quiet days and days when we both feel tired. But even on those days, I want you to know this: my love for you is not fragile. It does not depend on perfect weather or perfect moods. It is patient. It is steady. It is yours.

So this is my long note, Rumaysa. It is not enough, and it will never be enough, but it is true. I love you with the kind of love that grows quieter and deeper with time. I love you in the morning light and in the late hours. I love you in the small things and in the big ones. I love you simply because you are you — and that has always been more than enough.

Thank you for existing. Thank you for letting me love you. Thank you for being the person my heart keeps writing home to.

Always yours,
with all my heart
